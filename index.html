How I work

A simple process from brief to delivery.

    Clarify the goal, audience, platform, references and key message.

  2. Build the story

    Organize footage, select takes, cut a rough version and refine rhythm.

  3. Add the finish

    Graphics, titles, sound, color and effects, only where they help the story.

  4. Deliver

    Review together, make agreed revisions and export for your platform.
